# Notification event coverage

The durable in-app projection now includes SCRIPT_READY, VIDEO_FAILED and LIMIT_WARNING (migration 0072). Existing CONTENT_APPROVAL_REQUIRED and SOCIAL_TOKEN_EXPIRED represent the specification's APPROVAL_REQUIRED and TOKEN_EXPIRED events. Other existing events cover video readiness, publication results, payment results and subscription expiry. EMAIL and TELEGRAM notification delivery remain incomplete; authentication SMTP is a separate pipeline.

A successful GENERATE_SCRIPT event emits SCRIPT_READY to current editors/managers/admins/owners and CONTENT_APPROVAL_REQUIRED to approvers. An owner can receive both because creation and approval are distinct actions. Projection receipts deduplicate retries. A video failure or uncertain remote video result emits VIDEO_FAILED to editors and links to the video studio; it does not imply a retry is safe. Older already-projected generic notifications are preserved without retroactive duplicate delivery.

LIMIT_WARNING is visible only to current owners and links to billing. It is emitted when the available ledger balance for AI_CREDITS or VIDEO_SECONDS is at most 10% of that resource's latest GRANT or PURCHASE amount, including an exactly zero balance. Reservations reduce available balance; reserved work therefore can trigger the notice. No grant/purchase means no established budget and generates no warning. Refunds and adjustments affect balance but do not start a new warning cycle. A new grant/purchase starts a new cycle. One receipt per funding entry prevents repeated alerts; the two resources are independent. The 10% threshold is an initial product policy, not a requirement imposed by the specification.

This is a historical warning at projection time, not a live balance or an enforcement mechanism. Later releases or purchases can make it stale; the copy explicitly directs the user to the current billing balance. The ledger remains authoritative for spending authorization. Projection handles at most 25 low-balance funding entries per batch and scopes lookup by tenant when requested. Production-scale query latency and alert policy tuning remain to be measured.

Recipient membership and disabled-account state are checked during fan-out; current role and ownership are rechecked when listing or marking read. Notification content contains fixed safe copy and resource links, not provider errors, scripts, ledger amounts or audit payloads.
